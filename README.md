# AI-CRM: AI Email Verification & Data Prediction

**AI-CRM** is a powerful platform designed to verify email addresses and intelligently predict associated data, such as names, using advanced AI algorithms. Our goal is to help you clean your email lists, reduce bounce rates, enrich your contact data, and ultimately boost the deliverability and effectiveness of your email campaigns and sign-up forms.

Powered by **Dataoorts - AICRM Project**.

---

## ✨ Key Features

*   **🤖 AI-Powered Accuracy**: Leverages advanced algorithms for precise email validation, going beyond simple syntax checks.
*   **👤 Name Prediction**: Our AI model intelligently predicts the name of the email holder, enriching your contact data.
*   **⚡ Instant Results**: Get verification and prediction status in seconds. Supports both single email and batch processing.
*   **🛡️ Improve Deliverability**: Significantly reduce bounce rates and protect your sender reputation. Essential for effective sign-up forms and email marketing.
*   **🔗 Seamless Integration**: Easily integrate with your existing workflows via our powerful Async REST API.
*   **📂 Effortless Batch Processing**: Upload and process entire email lists asynchronously for verification and name prediction.
